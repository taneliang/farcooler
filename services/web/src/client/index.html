Add this browser to your runners

Your phone already reaches your runners. It can add this browser’s passkey to the runners you choose, and then your terminals open here.

  1. On your phone, open Far Cooler, tap Add Device or Runner, then Add Another Device.
  2. Scan this code, choose the runners this browser may use, and confirm.
  3. Your phone then shows a code of its own. Select Scan Reply and hold it up to this computer’s camera.
