Needs You and Rulings

You see only the calls that are yours, and you can undo the ones that were made for you.

Needs You

Needs You is one list of everything waiting on you, from every workspace on every runner, most urgent first:

  • An agent asking permission, such as to run a command. Answer with Allow or Deny.
  • A blocked agent, or one whose last turn failed. Open takes you to it.
  • A decision the orchestrator asked you for. Pick one of its answers, or write your own.
  • A pull request thread an agent couldn’t settle, and a pull request waiting on your review.
  • A task ready for review.

Your answer goes on the task, and the orchestrator picks it up from there.

On a shared runner, each row also says whose item it is: You, a teammate’s name, Owner, or Anyone. Answering someone else’s item asks “Answer for them?” first. Teams has the details.

Rulings: decided for you, undone in a click

Waiting for you on every small call would stall the work. So when a call can be undone, such as a default or a color, the orchestrator makes it, keeps the work moving, and records a ruling: what it decided, why, and what reversing it would cost.

Open rulings are under Decided For You in the plan:

  • Keep marks it kept. The orchestrator treats kept rulings as precedent.
  • Reverse and Discuss write a message to the orchestrator for you, which you send when you’re ready.

Calls that can’t be undone, or that reach outside the project, always wait for you: publishing, deleting, spending, and anything involving other people’s data.
